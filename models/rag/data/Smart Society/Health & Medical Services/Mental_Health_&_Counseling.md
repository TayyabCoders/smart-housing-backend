# 🧠 Mental Health & Counseling Services

Smart Society recognizes mental well-being as equally important as physical health. The following services are available for residents in a safe, confidential environment.

## 1. Counseling Sessions

| Service | Schedule | Location |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Individual Therapy | By appointment | Wellness Room, Block B |
| Couples Counseling | Wednesdays & Saturdays | Wellness Room, Block B |
| Teen & Youth Sessions | Fridays 4–6 PM | Community Hall |
| Group Support Circle | Every Sunday 6 PM | Library Lounge |

* To book a session, use the **Society App → Wellness → Book Counseling**.
* All sessions are **strictly confidential** and protected under the Society's Privacy Policy.

## 3. Support Programs

* **Mindfulness Monday:** Free 45-minute guided meditation session every Monday at 7 AM at the Grand Park Amphitheater.
* **Parenting Workshop:** Monthly workshop for parents on managing child behavior and screen-time.
* **Elder Care Support:** Dedicated bi-weekly group session for senior citizens at the Senior Citizen Lounge.
* **Stress-Free Fridays:** Light yoga and breathing exercises — open to all residents, free of charge.

## 4. Crisis Support

In case of a mental health crisis or emergency:
* Call the **24/7 Helpline: Ext. 188** from any society phone.
* Reach the on-call counselor at **+92-333-8800112**.
* The helpline is manned by trained staff and guarantees a response within 10 minutes.

All mental health records are kept separate from general medical records and are never shared without written consent.
