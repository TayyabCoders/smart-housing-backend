# 🚑 Ambulance & First Aid Services

## 1. Emergency Ambulance

The society operates two dedicated ambulances available around the clock for medical emergencies within the premises.

| Detail | Information |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Ambulance Hotline** | 4455 / +92-321-9876543 |
| **Availability** | 24 hours, 7 days a week |
| **Response Time Target** | Under 8 minutes within society limits |
| **Equipped With** | Oxygen cylinder, defibrillator, stretcher, first aid kit |

### How to Call an Ambulance
1. Dial **4455** from any landline inside the society or call the mobile number.
2. State your exact address, house number, and block.
3. Briefly describe the nature of the emergency.
4. Keep the line open — the dispatcher will guide you until the ambulance arrives.

All first aid centers are stocked with AED (Automated External Defibrillator) units, oxygen support, and trauma kits.

## 3. Trained First Responders

* A minimum of **two trained first-aid staff** are present at the Community Center at all times.
* Security guards at the main and secondary gates are trained in **CPR and basic first aid**.
* Residents are encouraged to enroll in the quarterly **First Aid & CPR Workshop** — check the Society App for the next session date.
