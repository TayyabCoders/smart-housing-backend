# 🏥 Society Clinic & Pharmacy

The Smart Society operates an in-house medical clinic and pharmacy to ensure residents have access to basic healthcare without leaving the premises.

## 1. Clinic Details

| Detail | Information |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Location** | Community Center, Block B, Ground Floor |
| **Operating Hours** | 8:00 AM – 10:00 PM (Daily) |
| **Emergency Line** | Ext. 199 / +92-300-1234567 |
| **Doctor on Duty** | Always present during operating hours |

## 2. Services Offered

* **General OPD** — Consultation for fever, flu, minor injuries, blood pressure, and diabetes monitoring.
* **Lab Tests** — Basic blood work (CBC, blood sugar, urine RE) available on-site.
* **Vaccination Clinic** — Routine vaccinations including flu shots and COVID boosters.
* **Mother & Child Health** — Prenatal and postnatal checkups every Tuesday and Thursday.
* **Telemedicine Referrals** — For specialist consultations, the clinic arranges video appointments.

## 3. Pharmacy

| Item Category | Availability |
| :--- | :--- |
| OTC Medicines | Always in stock |
| Prescription Drugs | Available with valid prescription |
| Surgical Supplies | Bandages, gloves, syringes |
| Medical Devices | BP monitors, glucometers (rental available) |

* **Pharmacy Hours:** 7:00 AM – 11:00 PM daily.
* Residents holding a **Society Medical Card** receive a **10% discount** on all pharmacy purchases.

## 4. Medical Card

Residents may apply for a Society Medical Card at the Admin Office. The card covers:
1. Subsidized OPD consultation fee (Rs. 150 instead of Rs. 300).
2. 10% discount on pharmacy bills.
3. Priority queue at the clinic counter.
