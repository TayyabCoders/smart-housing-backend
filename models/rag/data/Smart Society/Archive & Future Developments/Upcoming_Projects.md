## 2. Technology Upgrades

* **Fiber-to-the-Home (FTTH):** Upgrading all 1,248 units to dedicated high-speed fiber internet (minimum 100 Mbps).
* **Automated Street Lighting:** 420 motion-sensing LED lights to replace existing sodium vapor lamps — reducing electricity consumption by an estimated 38%.
* **AI-Powered CCTV:** Upgrade of 60 key cameras with AI-based face recognition and anomaly detection (subject to resident approval and privacy audit).
* **Smart Meter Rollout:** Replacing all sub-meters with smart meters that feed real-time consumption data to the resident portal.
* **Society App v3.0:** Major update including: parcel tracking, smart home deeper integration, in-app community forum, and resident voting for committee decisions.

## 3. Green & Sustainability Initiatives

* **Rainwater Harvesting System:** Pilot installation at the Community Center and Grand Park to reduce water consumption.
* **Composting Station:** Expansion of the recycling center to include organic waste composting — output will be used for society's gardens.
* **Electric Shuttle Upgrade:** Replacing 2 of the 3 internal shuttles with fully electric models.
* **Urban Farming Plots:** 20 small raised-bed farming plots available to interested residents in the Green Belt area.

## 4. How to Give Input

Residents are encouraged to participate in shaping upcoming projects:
1. Attend the **Quarterly Town Hall** (announced via Society App — usually first Saturday of January, April, July, October).
2. Submit suggestions via **Society App → Community → Suggest a Project**.
3. Vote on shortlisted proposals during the annual **Resident Survey** each November.
