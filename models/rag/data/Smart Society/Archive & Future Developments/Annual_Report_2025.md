# 📰 Smart Society Annual Report — 2025

## 1. Message from the Admin Committee

2025 marked a landmark year for Smart Society. We expanded our amenities, upgraded security infrastructure, and maintained a 100% maintenance collection rate in Q3 and Q4. This report summarizes key achievements, challenges faced, and the path forward.

## 3. Key Achievements of 2025

* **CCTV Upgrade:** 48 new PTZ cameras installed across all streets and parking zones — total camera count now stands at 214.
* **Gym Expansion:** The fitness center was expanded by 1,200 sq. ft. adding a dedicated functional training zone.
* **Digital Portal Launch:** The Resident Online Portal (portal.smartsociety.pk) was launched in March 2025, reducing in-person admin visits by 40%.
* **Green Initiative:** 1,400 new trees and shrubs planted as part of the annual plantation drive.
* **Sports Gala 2025:** 320 residents participated across 6 sports disciplines — the highest participation to date.
* **Solar Pilot (Phase 0):** 60 kWp solar installation on the community center roof — saving Rs. 6.2 lakh in electricity costs.

## 4. Challenges & How They Were Addressed

| Challenge | Resolution |
| :--- | :--- |
| Maintenance arrears in Block C | Personal follow-up by Admin + payment plan options introduced |
| Unauthorized construction (3 cases) | Stop-work orders issued; 2 cases regularized; 1 referred to legal committee |
| Drainage blockage in Sector B (monsoon) | Emergency desilting contracted; long-term drainage upgrade funded from development reserve |
| Noise complaints (late-night events) | Stricter enforcement of Event NOC; security patrols increased on weekends |
