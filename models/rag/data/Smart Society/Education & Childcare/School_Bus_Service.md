# 🚌 School Bus Service

## 1. Overview

Smart Society operates a dedicated school bus service for resident children attending schools within a 15 km radius. All buses are GPS-tracked, air-conditioned, and staffed with a trained bus attendant.

| Route | Schools Covered | Morning Departure | Afternoon Return |

## 4. GPS Tracking & Safety

* Parents can track the bus **live** via the Society App → School Bus → Live Track.
* An **automated SMS** is sent to parents when the bus departs from school and when it reaches the society gate.
* Children are only dropped off to an authorized adult — any change to the pickup person must be communicated to the bus coordinator at least 24 hours in advance.

## 5. Bus Conduct Rules for Students

1. Students must be at the boarding point **5 minutes before** scheduled departure. The bus will not wait more than 2 minutes.
2. Seatbelts must be worn at all times while the bus is moving.
3. No food or littering on the bus.
4. Loud music, fighting, or disrespect toward the driver or attendant results in suspension from the service.
5. The bus attendant's instructions must be followed at all times.
