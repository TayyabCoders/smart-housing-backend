# 📚 Tuition & Learning Center

## 1. Overview

The Smart Society Learning Center provides subsidized academic support to resident children from Grade 1 through Intermediate (Grade 12). The center is staffed by qualified educators and operates six days a week.

* **Scholarship Program:** Top 5 students from the quarterly assessment exam receive **3 months free tuition**.

## 4. Enrollment

1. Visit the Education Block or use the **Society App → Education → Enroll Child**.
2. Bring the child's latest school report card and CNIC copy of parent.
3. An informal placement test is conducted to assign the correct batch.

## 5. Rules for Students

1. Punctuality is mandatory — 3 late arrivals count as one absence.
2. Mobile phones must be switched off inside the learning center.
3. Parents must collect students within 15 minutes of session end. Repeated delays will be flagged.
4. Damage to furniture or equipment will be charged to the parent's society account.
