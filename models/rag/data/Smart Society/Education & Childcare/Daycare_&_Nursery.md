# 👶 Daycare & Nursery

## 1. Overview

Smart Society's licensed daycare and nursery facility caters to children from **6 months to 5 years of age**, providing a safe, stimulating, and nurturing environment for the youngest residents.

## 4. Safety & Health Policies

1. **Authorized Pickup Only:** Children can only be collected by individuals listed on the authorized pickup form. A valid ID must be shown.
2. **Sick Child Policy:** Children with fever (above 38°C), vomiting, or contagious conditions must stay home and may return 24 hours after symptoms clear.
3. **Allergy Register:** All known food allergies must be declared at enrollment. The kitchen maintains a strict allergen-free policy for affected children.
4. **CCTV Monitoring:** The daycare is fully monitored via CCTV. Parents may request live-feed access via the Society App.

## 5. Enrollment

* Submit the **Daycare Enrollment Form** at the Admin Office or via the Society App.
* A one-time registration fee of **Rs. 1,500** applies.
* A medical certificate from a licensed doctor confirming the child's health is required at enrollment.
