# 🏛️ Property Tax & Society Dues Guide

This document outlines the tax obligations for property owners as per government regulations and society bylaws.

## 1. Types of Taxes & Levies

| Tax Type | Frequency | Calculation Basis |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Provincial Property Tax** | Annual | Plot size and rental value |
| **Society Development Cess** | One-time | Applied at the time of construction |
| **Water & Conservancy Tax** | Monthly | Fixed based on plot category |
| **Capital Value Tax (CVT)** | One-time (on purchase) | % of declared property value |
| **Withholding Tax on Sale** | At point of sale | 1% of sale value (filer) / 2% (non-filer) |

## 4. Payment Deadlines

* **Early Bird Discount:** 5% discount if the annual provincial property tax is paid before **September 30th** of each year.
* **Society Maintenance Due Date:** 10th of every month — online payment preferred via Society App or portal.
* **Annual Development Dues:** Payable in July each year; installment plan available (apply at Admin Office by June 30th).

## 5. Exemptions & Rebates

* **Widows and orphan-headed households:** Full exemption from society maintenance for the first 5 Marla unit; 50% on any additional units.
* **Disabled persons (CNIC certified):** 25% rebate on monthly maintenance dues.
* **Senior Citizens (65+) on fixed income:** 15% discount on maintenance — apply with proof of pension or income statement.
* **Early Payment Reward:** Residents who pay 12 months of maintenance in a single upfront payment receive a **7% discount**.

## 6. How to Pay

| Payment Method | Details |
| :--- | :--- |
| Society App | App → My Bills → Maintenance → Pay |
| Online Portal | portal.smartsociety.pk → Bills |
| Cash / Cheque | Admin Office counter, Mon–Sat 9 AM – 4 PM |
| Bank Transfer | Account details provided on bill (add house number as reference) |

## 7. Tax Documentation

The society provides the following documents upon request:
* **Property Tax Payment Certificate** (for bank loan applications).
* **Maintenance Clearance Letter** (required for property transfer NOC).
* **Annual Statement of Account** (for audit or personal records).

Request via **Society App → Documents → Request Certificate** or Admin Office. Processing time: 3 working days.
