# 💡 Utility Bills & Payment Guide

## 1. Overview

All utility bills (electricity, gas, and water) within Smart Society are managed through a sub-metering system. Residents receive itemized monthly bills via the Society App and by physical delivery to their letterbox.

## 2. Bill Generation Schedule

| Utility | Meter Reading Date | Bill Issuance | Due Date | Late Fee |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Electricity | 1st of each month | 5th of each month | 15th of each month | Rs. 200 flat + 1.5% per week |
| Gas | 1st of each month | 5th of each month | 15th of each month | Rs. 150 flat |
| Water (Society Supply) | 28th of previous month | 3rd of each month | 12th of each month | Rs. 100 flat |

## 3. Electricity Rate Slab (Society Internal Grid)

| Units Consumed (Monthly) | Rate per Unit |
| :--- | :--- |
| 1 – 100 units | Rs. 22 |
| 101 – 300 units | Rs. 28 |
| 301 – 700 units | Rs. 34 |
| Above 700 units | Rs. 42 |

A **fixed service charge of Rs. 300** is added to every electricity bill regardless of consumption.

## 4. Payment Methods

| Method | How to Pay |
| :--- | :--- |
| Society App | App → My Bills → Pay Now |
| Online Portal | portal.smartsociety.pk → Bills |
| Bank Transfer | Account details on the bill (reference: your house number) |
| EasyPaisa / JazzCash | Bill number available in the app |
| Cash (Admin Office) | Counter open Mon–Sat, 9 AM – 5 PM |

## 5. Disputed Bills

* If a resident believes their bill is incorrect, a **Bill Dispute Form** must be submitted within **5 days of bill issuance**.
* The finance team will re-verify the meter reading and respond within **3 working days**.
* During the dispute period, the previous month's bill amount is payable to avoid late fees.

## 6. Solar Credit Policy

Residents with **approved solar panels** (see Infrastructure Policy) may generate excess electricity. This excess is logged and credited to the next month's electricity bill at **Rs. 18 per unit**.

## 7. Disconnection Policy

| Arrears Duration | Action |
| :--- | :--- |
| 15 days past due | Reminder SMS + App notification |
| 30 days past due | Written notice from Admin Office |
| 45 days past due | Utility disconnection (reconnection fee: Rs. 500) |
| 90 days past due | Escalation to society legal committee |
