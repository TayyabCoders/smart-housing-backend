# ⚖️ Dispute Resolution & Legal Committee

## 1. Types of Disputes Handled

The Arbitration Committee handles resident disputes including but not limited to:
* Noise complaints and late-night disturbances.
* Parking space encroachment.
* Boundary wall and construction disputes.
* Tree/branch encroachment onto neighboring property.
* Shared wall damage claims.
* Harassment or misconduct between neighbors.

## 2. Dispute Resolution Procedure

1. **Submit a Written Complaint** to the Admin Office (in person or via the Society App → Complaints → Neighbor Dispute).
2. The Committee will **acknowledge receipt within 48 hours** and assign a case number.
3. Both parties are **summoned within 7 working days** for an initial hearing.
4. The Committee may conduct a **site inspection** if the dispute involves physical property (construction, boundary, parking).
5. A **resolution hearing** is held within 14 days of the initial summons.
6. The Committee's **written decision is issued within 3 days** of the final hearing.
7. The decision is **final and binding** on both parties within society premises.

## 3. Arbitration Committee Composition

| Role | Title |
| :--- | :--- |
| Chairperson | Elected Resident Representative |
| Legal Advisor | Licensed Advocate (contracted by society) |
| Admin Representative | Society Admin Manager |
| Neutral Resident Member | Randomly selected from a panel of volunteers |

## 4. Appeals

* If a party disagrees with the Committee's decision, they may file a formal appeal in writing within **10 days** of the decision.
* Appeals are reviewed by the **Senior Advisory Board** (a separate three-member panel).
* The Senior Advisory Board's ruling is final at the society level.
* Parties retain the right to escalate to **civil courts** for matters not resolved internally, though the society encourages internal resolution first.

## 5. Legal Matters & External Disputes

| Situation | Society's Role |
| :--- | :--- |
| Property fraud or title dispute | Provides documented evidence; does not adjudicate |
| Criminal complaints (theft, assault) | Cooperates fully with police; CCTV footage provided |
| Court-ordered notices served to a resident | Admin Office receives and forwards formally |

## 6. Fines for Non-Compliance with Committee Decisions

* Failure to comply with a Committee decision within the stipulated timeframe: **Rs. 5,000 per week**.
* Repeated non-compliance: escalation to the society's Legal Counsel for formal action.
* RFID access privileges may be suspended pending compliance.
