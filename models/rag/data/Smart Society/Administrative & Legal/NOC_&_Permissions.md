# 📋 NOC & Permission Request Guide

## 1. What is a Society NOC?

A No Objection Certificate (NOC) from the Smart Society Admin Office is required for certain activities within the premises. Without it, the activity may be halted and a fine imposed.

## 2. Activities Requiring an NOC

| Activity | NOC Type | Processing Time | Fee |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Home renovation / construction | Construction NOC | 5 working days | Rs. 1,500 |
| Shifting in / shifting out | Move-In / Move-Out NOC | 2 working days | Free |
| Installing solar panels | Infrastructure NOC | 7 working days | Rs. 2,000 |
| Setting up a home-based business | Commercial Use NOC | 7 working days | Rs. 3,000 |
| Hosting a private event (>50 guests) | Event NOC | 3 working days | Rs. 500 |
| Installing a generator | Generator NOC | 4 working days | Rs. 1,000 |
| Keeping commercial livestock / birds | Special Permission | 10 working days | Rs. 2,500 |
| Digging or boring a well | Drilling NOC | 10 working days | Rs. 5,000 |

## 3. How to Apply

1. Download the **NOC Application Form** from the Society App or online portal.
2. Fill in the form completely and attach supporting documents (listed in the form checklist).
3. Submit at the **Admin Office Counter (Mon–Sat, 9 AM – 4 PM)** or upload via the portal.
4. Pay the applicable fee (if any) at the finance counter or via online payment.
5. You will receive an **SMS notification** once the NOC is approved or if additional information is needed.

## 4. Common Rejection Reasons

* Incomplete documentation (missing floor plan, contractor license, etc.).
* Outstanding maintenance dues on the property.
* Proposed activity violates society bylaws.
* Adjacenet neighbor objection filed within the review period.

## 5. NOC Validity

| NOC Type | Valid For |
| :--- | :--- |
| Construction NOC | Duration of approved construction (max 12 months) |
| Solar Panel NOC | Lifetime of the installation |
| Event NOC | Date of event only |
| Home Business NOC | 1 year (annual renewal required) |

## 6. Penalties for Non-Compliance

| Violation | Fine |
| :--- | :--- |
| Construction without NOC | Rs. 10,000 + work stoppage |
| Event without NOC (>50 guests) | Rs. 5,000 |
| Solar panel without NOC | Rs. 8,000 + removal notice |
| Generator without NOC | Rs. 3,000 |
