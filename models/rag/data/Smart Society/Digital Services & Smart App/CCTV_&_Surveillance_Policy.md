# 📷 CCTV & Surveillance Policy

## 1. Overview

Smart Society operates a comprehensive CCTV surveillance network across all common areas, entry/exit points, and key infrastructure zones. This policy defines how footage is managed, accessed, and protected.

## 2. Camera Coverage

| Zone | Camera Type | Coverage |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Main Gate & Secondary Gates | PTZ + Fixed | 24/7 recording |
| Society Roads & Streets | Fixed wide-angle | 24/7 recording |
| Parking Areas | Fixed + License Plate Reader | 24/7 recording |
| Community Center & Lobby | Fixed dome | 24/7 recording |
| Sports Complex | Fixed | During operating hours |
| Playground & Parks | Fixed | 24/7 recording |
| Lifts & Elevator Lobbies | Fixed dome | 24/7 recording |

> **Note:** CCTV cameras are NOT installed inside residential units, prayer rooms, washrooms, or changing areas.

## 3. Footage Retention

* All footage is stored on **encrypted local servers** within the society premises for **30 days**.
* After 30 days, footage is automatically overwritten unless flagged for a legal or disciplinary matter.
* Footage flagged for an incident is retained for **up to 6 months** or until the matter is resolved.

## 4. Access Policy

* CCTV footage is accessible only to authorized **Security Staff, Admin Committee, and Law Enforcement** (with written request).
* Residents may **request a review** of footage involving their property or a reported incident by submitting a written application to the Admin Office.
* Resident requests will be reviewed and responded to within **3 working days**.
* Footage is never shared publicly or with unauthorized third parties.

## 5. Resident Home Camera Integration

Residents who install private cameras must adhere to the following:
1. Cameras may only face your own property — not a neighbor's entrance, window, or private space.
2. Cameras facing shared roads or common areas must be registered with the Admin Office.
3. Misuse of private cameras (e.g., voyeurism) is a criminal offense and will be reported to police.

## 6. Reporting Surveillance Issues

| Issue | Contact |
| :--- | :--- |
| Camera vandalism or tampering | Security Control Room: Ext. 911 |
| Privacy concern / complaint | Admin Office: Ext. 100 |
| Technical failure of CCTV | IT Helpdesk: Ext. 150 |
