# 💻 Resident Online Portal — User Guide

## 1. Portal Overview

The Smart Society Resident Portal (portal.smartsociety.pk) is the web-based dashboard for managing all society-related services. It complements the mobile Society App and is best accessed from a computer for detailed tasks.

## 2. Account Setup

1. Visit **portal.smartsociety.pk** and click **"New Resident Registration."**
2. Enter your **CNIC number** and **House/Plot number** — these are verified against the society database.
3. Set a password and confirm your email address.
4. A verification OTP will be sent to your registered mobile number.
5. Log in with your credentials to access the full dashboard.

> If you face any issue during registration, contact the IT Helpdesk at **Ext. 150** or email **support@smartsociety.pk**.

## 3. Portal Features

| Feature | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Bill Payment** | Pay maintenance, utility, and special levies online |
| **Complaint Submission** | Log and track complaints in real time |
| **Document Downloads** | Download NOC, property receipts, and tax documents |
| **Visitor Pre-Registration** | Pre-approve guests before their arrival |
| **Community Notices** | Read official announcements from the Admin |
| **Booking System** | Book sports courts, guest rooms, and common areas |
| **CCTV Access** | View your street-facing camera feed (subject to approval) |

## 4. Bill Payment Steps

1. Log in → **My Account → Bills & Payments**.
2. Select the bill type (maintenance, utility, parking, etc.).
3. Choose payment method: **Debit/Credit Card, EasyPaisa, JazzCash, or Bank Transfer**.
4. Review the amount and click **Pay Now**.
5. A payment receipt is emailed and available for download under **My Documents**.

## 5. Complaint Tracking

* After submitting a complaint, you receive a **Complaint ID** (e.g., SS-2026-04521).
* Track status via **My Complaints → Enter Complaint ID**.
* Status stages: `Submitted → Assigned → In Progress → Resolved`.
* You will receive an SMS at each status change.

## 6. Portal Access Levels

| Role | Access |
| :--- | :--- |
| Resident (Owner) | Full access to all features |
| Tenant | Bill payment, complaints, bookings — no legal documents |
| Property Manager | View-only for bills and complaints for their properties |
| Admin Staff | Back-end management panel (separate login) |
