# 🚴 Jogging Tracks & Cycling Lanes

## 1. Track Layout

Smart Society features a dedicated **2.4 km looped jogging track** and a parallel **cycling lane** that circles the society's inner green belt.

| Track Section | Distance | Surface |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Main Jogging Loop | 2.4 km | Rubberized anti-slip |
| Inner Walking Path | 1.1 km | Paved stone |
| Cycling Lane | 2.4 km | Asphalt with lane markings |
| Children's Bicycle Path | 0.6 km | Flat rubberized surface |

## 2. Operating Hours

The tracks are open **24 hours** but proper lighting is active from **5:00 AM – 10:00 PM**. Jogging after 10 PM is at the resident's own discretion.

## 3. Rules & Etiquette

1. **Direction of travel:** All joggers move **counter-clockwise**; cyclists move **clockwise** on their dedicated lane.
2. Cyclists must **ring bell or call out** before overtaking a jogger near any shared crossing point.
3. The maximum cycling speed on the path is **15 km/h**.
4. Earphones are permitted but residents must remain aware of their surroundings.
5. Pets on leash are allowed on the walking path only — not on the jogging or cycling lanes.
6. **No electric scooters or motor bikes** on any pedestrian or cycling path.

## 4. Facilities Along the Track

| Facility | Location on Loop |
| :--- | :--- |
| Water Drinking Stations | At 0.6 km, 1.2 km, and 2.0 km marks |
| Rest Benches | Every 300 meters |
| Emergency SOS Button | At 1.0 km and 2.0 km marks |
| Public Restrooms | Near the Grand Park entrance |

## 5. Bicycle Rental

Residents who do not own a bicycle may rent one from the Sports Desk:

| Duration | Fee |
| :--- | :--- |
| 1 hour | Rs. 100 |
| Half day (4 hours) | Rs. 300 |
| Full day | Rs. 500 |

A refundable deposit of Rs. 1,000 is required at the time of rental.
