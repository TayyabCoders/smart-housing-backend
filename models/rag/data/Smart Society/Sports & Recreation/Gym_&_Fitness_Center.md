# 💪 Gym & Fitness Center

## 1. Overview

The Smart Society Fitness Center is a fully equipped gym available exclusively to registered residents and their immediate family members.

| Detail | Information |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Location** | Sports Complex, Block D, 2nd Floor |
| **Area** | 5,000 sq. ft. |
| **Operating Hours** | 5:00 AM – 11:00 PM daily |
| **Helpdesk** | Ext. 240 |

## 2. Equipment Available

* **Cardio Zone:** 12 treadmills, 6 ellipticals, 4 stationary bikes, 2 rowing machines.
* **Free Weights Zone:** Dumbbells (2 kg – 50 kg), barbells, kettlebells, bench press stations.
* **Machine Zone:** Leg press, lat pulldown, cable crossover, chest fly, leg curl/extension machines.
* **Functional Training Zone:** TRX bands, battle ropes, plyometric boxes, resistance bands.
* **Stretching Area:** Yoga mats, foam rollers, balance balls.

## 6. Gym Rules

1. Wipe down equipment after use — sanitizer spray and towels are provided at each station.
2. Proper gym attire (sports shoes mandatory) is required at all times.
3. **Re-rack weights** after use. Leaving weights on the floor is subject to a verbal warning, repeated violations result in a 7-day membership suspension.
4. Mobile phones in silent mode; video calls and loud calls are not permitted on the gym floor.
5. Lockers are available — bring your own lock or rent one at the front desk for Rs. 50/day.
