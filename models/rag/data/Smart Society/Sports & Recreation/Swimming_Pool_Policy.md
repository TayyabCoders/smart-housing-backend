# 🏊 Swimming Pool — Policy & Guidelines

## 1. Pool Overview

| Detail | Information |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Location** | Sports Complex, Block D (Rear Section) |
| **Pool Dimensions** | 25m × 12m (Olympic-standard lanes) |
| **Depth** | Shallow end: 1.0m / Deep end: 2.4m |
| **Children's Wading Pool** | Adjacent — max depth 0.6m |
| **Lifeguards on Duty** | At all times during operating hours |

## 2. Operating Hours & Sessions

| Session | Time | Eligible Residents |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Morning Lap Swimming | 5:30 AM – 8:00 AM | Adults only |
| Family Session | 9:00 AM – 12:00 PM | All residents |
| Ladies Only Session | 2:00 PM – 4:00 PM | Female residents |
| Evening Open Swim | 5:00 PM – 9:00 PM | All residents |

## 3. Entry & Access

* Pool access requires scanning your **RFID resident card** at the pool gate.
* Guests may use the pool only during **Family Sessions** and must be accompanied by a registered resident.
* Guest pool pass: Rs. 300 per visit (payable at the Sports Desk).

## 4. Pool Rules

1. **Swimming attire is mandatory** — street clothes and denim are strictly prohibited in the pool.
2. Shower before entering the pool.
3. No running, diving from the sides, or pushing others.
4. Children under 12 must be accompanied by an adult in the main pool at all times.
5. No food, chewing gum, or glass items near the pool area.
6. **Residents with communicable skin conditions** must refrain from using the pool until cleared by a doctor.

## 5. Lessons & Coaching

| Program | Schedule | Fee (Monthly) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Beginner Swimming (Kids 5–12) | Sat & Sun, 10 AM | Rs. 2,000 |
| Adult Beginner Course | Mon/Wed/Fri, 6 AM | Rs. 2,500 |

To enroll, contact the **Sports Desk (Ext. 230)** or register via the Society App.

## 6. Safety & Violations

| Violation | Consequence |
| :--- | :--- |
| Ignoring lifeguard instructions | Immediate removal from pool |
| Fighting or dangerous behavior | 30-day pool ban |
| Bringing unauthorized guests | Rs. 1,000 fine + guest ban |
