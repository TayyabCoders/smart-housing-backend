# 🏟️ Sports Complex — Rules & Regulations

The Smart Society Sports Complex is a state-of-the-art facility located in Block D, offering a range of indoor and outdoor sporting options for residents of all ages.

## 1. Facilities Available

| Facility | Location | Capacity |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Cricket Ground | Block D, Outer Ground | 22 players + spectators |
| Football/Futsal Court | Block D, South Wing | 10 players per side |
| Basketball Court | Block D, North Wing | 5 players per side |
| Badminton Hall | Sports Complex, 1st Floor | 4 courts |
| Table Tennis Room | Sports Complex, Ground Floor | 6 tables |
| Squash Court | Sports Complex, Basement | 2 courts |

## 2. Booking Policy

* All courts and grounds must be **booked in advance** via the Society App under **Recreation → Sports Booking**.
* Walk-in access is allowed only if the facility is unoccupied.
* Maximum booking duration: **2 hours per session** per household.
* Residents may hold a maximum of **2 active bookings** at any given time.

## 3. Operating Hours

| Day | Hours |
| :--- | :--- |
| Monday – Friday | 6:00 AM – 10:00 PM |
| Saturday – Sunday | 5:30 AM – 11:00 PM |
| Public Holidays | 6:00 AM – 9:00 PM |

## 4. Rules of Conduct

1. Appropriate sports attire and footwear are mandatory inside all courts.
2. **No food or drinks** (other than water) are allowed on the playing surfaces.
3. Loud music, profanity, and unsportsmanlike behavior will result in immediate expulsion and a temporary ban.
4. Children under 10 must be accompanied by an adult at all times.
5. Residents are responsible for the equipment they use — any damage will be billed to the resident's account.

## 5. Annual Society Sports Tournament

* The society hosts an annual **Sports Gala** every March.
* Events include cricket, football, badminton, and table tennis.
* Registration opens in January via the Society App.
* Winning teams receive trophies and society vouchers worth Rs. 5,000 – Rs. 15,000.
