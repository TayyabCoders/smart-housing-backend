# 🚗 Parking Management Policy

## 1. Overview

Smart Society has a structured parking management system to ensure all residents, visitors, and commercial tenants have access to safe and organized parking.

## 2. Residential Parking

| Plot Size | Allocated Parking Bays | Location |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 5 Marla | 1 dedicated bay | Garage (inside plot) |
| 10 Marla | 2 dedicated bays | Garage + 1 on-street reserved bay |
| 1 Kanal | 3 dedicated bays | Garage + 2 on-street reserved bays |
| Apartment (1 & 2 bed) | 1 basement parking slot | Designated basement level |
| Apartment (3 bed & above) | 2 basement parking slots | Designated basement level |

* Parking bays are assigned at the time of possession and are **non-transferable**.
* If a resident does not own a vehicle, they may **lease their parking bay** to another resident with Admin Office approval.

## 3. Visitor Parking

| Location | Capacity | Hours |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Main Gate Visitor Lot | 40 vehicles | 24/7 |
| Community Center Lot | 25 vehicles | 7 AM – 11 PM |
| Commercial Area Lot | 60 vehicles | 6 AM – 12 AM |
| Sports Complex Lot | 35 vehicles | During facility hours |

* Visitor parking is **free for the first 3 hours**.
* Beyond 3 hours: Rs. 50 per hour (deducted from resident's account if they invited the guest).

## 4. Parking Rules

1. **No double parking** at any time — first offense: Rs. 1,000 fine; repeat: vehicle towing at owner's cost.
2. Vehicles parked in fire lanes, disabled bays (without permit), or emergency access zones will be towed immediately.
3. No parking on footpaths, speed bumps, or grass verges.
4. Residents must display their **vehicle sticker** on the windshield — stickers are issued at the Admin Office.
5. Heavy vehicles (trucks, buses) are not permitted inside the society except during permitted construction hours.

## 5. Vehicle Registration

All resident vehicles must be registered with the society:
1. Visit the Admin Office with vehicle registration documents and CNIC.
2. A vehicle sticker and RFID tag will be issued.
3. The RFID tag enables **automatic gate opening** without stopping for guards.
4. Maximum 3 vehicles per household.

## 6. Fine Schedule

| Violation | Fine |
| :--- | :--- |
| No vehicle sticker | Rs. 500 |
| Parking in a disabled bay without permit | Rs. 3,000 |
| Double parking | Rs. 1,000 (1st) / Rs. 2,500 (repeat) |
| Blocking a fire lane | Rs. 5,000 + towing |
| Exceeding visitor parking time without extension | Rs. 50/hour |
