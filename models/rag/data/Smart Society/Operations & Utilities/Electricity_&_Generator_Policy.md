# ⚡ Electricity Supply & Generator Policy

## 1. Society Electricity Infrastructure

Smart Society operates on a dedicated sub-station supplied by the national grid, supplemented by two 500 kVA backup generators for common areas and a growing solar grid.

| Supply Type | Capacity | Coverage |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Main WAPDA Grid | Dedicated 11 kV feeder | All residential and commercial units |
| Backup Generator Set A | 500 kVA | Street lights, gate systems, community center |
| Backup Generator Set B | 500 kVA | Security infrastructure, water pumps, hospital |
| Solar Grid (Phase 1) | 200 kWp | Common area daytime load (from Q3 2026) |

## 2. Load Shedding Schedule

The society maintains a load shedding schedule that differs from the national grid:

| Time Slot | Society Load Shedding |
| :--- | :--- |
| 12:00 AM – 5:00 AM | Max 1 hour (non-peak, grid-dependent) |
| 5:00 AM – 10:00 PM | No scheduled load shedding |
| 10:00 PM – 12:00 AM | Up to 1 hour if grid supply is disrupted |

> During extended national grid outages, the backup generators power critical infrastructure only — not individual homes.

## 3. Private Generator Rules

Residents who wish to install their own home generators must comply with the following:

1. **NOC Required:** Obtain a Generator NOC from the Admin Office before installation (fee: Rs. 1,000).
2. **Generator Size Limit:** Max 10 kVA for 5 Marla plots; 20 kVA for 10 Marla; 25 kVA for 1 Kanal.
3. **Exhaust Requirements:** Generators must have an exhaust pipe routed to the exterior of the building, directed upward — not toward neighbor walls or windows.
4. **Operating Hours:** Home generators must not run between **11 PM and 6 AM** due to noise regulations.
5. **Fuel Storage:** On-premises fuel storage is limited to **50 liters** in an approved container. Larger quantities require special permission.

## 4. Solar Panel Policy

| Requirement | Detail |
| :--- | :--- |
| NOC Required | Yes — Infrastructure NOC (Rs. 2,000 fee) |
| Maximum Capacity | 10 kWp for residential |
| Grid Tie-In | Allowed — excess units credited to next bill at Rs. 18/unit |
| Approved Installers | Must be from the society's approved vendor list |
| Inspection Required | Post-installation inspection by society engineer mandatory |

Unapproved solar installations will be disconnected from the grid and a Rs. 8,000 fine imposed.

## 5. Electrical Fault Reporting

| Issue | Contact | Response Time |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Power outage (entire street/block) | Helpdesk Ext. 101 | Within 2 hours |
| Trip / indoor electrical fault | Helpdesk Ext. 101 | Within 4 hours |
| Sparking wire / dangerous fault | Emergency Line 112 | Immediate |
| Meter dispute | Finance Dept. Ext. 120 | Within 3 working days |
