# 🚨 Emergency Response & Safety SOP

## 1. Fire Safety

* Every household is recommended to maintain a fire extinguisher (minimum 2 kg ABC dry powder type).
* In case of fire, **evacuate immediately** and proceed to the designated **Assembly Point** (marked with green signs at every street intersection).
* Call the **Fire Emergency Line: Ext. 911** and **Rescue 1122** simultaneously.
* Do NOT use elevators during a fire — use stairwells only.

| Fire Safety Equipment | Location |
| :--- | :--- |
| Fire Extinguishers (community) | Every 50 meters on all main streets |
| Fire Hydrants | At all block entry points |
| Smoke Alarms | Community Center, Sports Complex, Education Block |
| Assembly Points | Main Gate, Block C Park, Block D Round-About |

## 2. Medical Emergency

* The society's 24/7 Ambulance service is available at **Ext. 4455 / +92-321-9876543**.
* The primary First Aid center is located at the **Community Center, Block B, Ground Floor**.
* Additional first aid kits are available at all security guard posts.
* For cardiac emergencies, AED units are located at: Community Center, Sports Complex main entrance, and Grand Park kiosk.

## 3. Security Threat / Intrusion

1. Call the **Security Control Room immediately: Ext. 911**.
2. Lock your doors and do not confront any intruder.
3. If safe to do so, take note of any description (clothing, vehicle, direction of movement) to report to guards.
4. In extreme emergencies, call **Police: 15** in parallel.
* All gates go into **lockdown mode** automatically when a Code Red is declared by the Security Head.

## 4. Gas Leak

1. Do NOT switch on or off any electrical appliance.
2. Open all windows and doors immediately.
3. Evacuate the house and call **Sui Gas Emergency: 1199** and **Society Emergency: Ext. 112**.
4. Do NOT re-enter until cleared by the gas department technician.

## 5. Flood / Waterlogging

* Report blocked drains to **Sanitation Dept: Ext. 105** immediately.
* In case of basement flooding, the society's emergency pump team (Ext. 112) responds within 30 minutes.
* Ground floor residents in affected sectors should move valuables to upper levels as a precaution during heavy monsoon rains.
